Review warehouse QC

When quality-control photos are available, check the color, size label, visible condition, and any measurements you requested. Raise discrepancies before authorizing shipment and check the return deadline for that order.

HipoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the two main buttons take me?

Both open the CAFANS homepage. The product links in the catalog open individual CAFANS listings, where you can look for the source listing and available shopping-agent options.

What is included in the shortlist CSV?

The file contains the selected product names, categories, reference USD prices, and CAFANS URLs. It also includes empty columns for the variant and QC notes so you can update the sheet as you compare listings.

Why can the final order total change?

The selected variant, exchange rate, parcel dimensions, and shipping route can affect the total. Use the checkout amount and your current parcel quote when making the final comparison.
